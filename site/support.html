Common Questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Sign in with the same account, then tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings → Subscription in the app and tap Manage Subscription, or go to your Apple ID’s Subscriptions settings on your iPhone.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password and follow the link in the email.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account removes your account and all of its data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
